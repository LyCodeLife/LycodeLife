Frequently Asked Questions about Automation

Resolving your technical doubts regarding our methodology and AI integration.

Who is behind LycodeLife?
LycodeLife was founded and is led by Shordy Simbaña, a Systems Engineer specializing in solving business bottlenecks with code. My goal is for your team to stop doing repetitive work and focus on adding real value while background scripts handle the rest.

What type of business processes can be automated?
Almost any digital repetitive task. Common cases include: bulk web data extraction (Scraping), intelligent PDF reading (OCR), sending bulk notifications via WhatsApp, and synchronizing software that doesn't communicate natively (e.g., connecting your e-commerce to your ERP).

How is Artificial Intelligence (AI) implemented in my company?
We integrate AI directly into your daily processes through code (using APIs like OpenAI). This allows your automations to "understand" context: from analyzing whether an incoming email is urgent, to extracting messy data from an invoice and organizing it perfectly into an Excel file without human supervision.

Is it safe to automate processes using my company's confidential data?
Absolutely. As an engineer, information security is my top priority. The scripts I develop are configured to run on your own infrastructure (whether local servers or your private cloud). I do not store, retain, or share commercial information or your customer databases.

How long does it take to develop a custom automation?
It depends on technical complexity and required integrations, but an average development (like a WhatsApp bot or data extractor) takes between 2 and 4 weeks from audit to final deployment. The best part is that the Return on Investment (ROI) is perceived immediately by eliminating manual work hours from day one.
